About

Arthur Valente is a Brazilian architectural visualization artist working with architects worldwide. He has held senior positions at MIR (Bergen, Norway) and Brick Visual (Budapest), two of the most respected studios in the field. His work spans residential, commercial, and competition projects, with a focus on atmosphere, light, and landscape. He works primarily with 3ds Max, V-Ray, and Corona.
